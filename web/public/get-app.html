Install on Android

Three steps

1. Download

Tap Download for Android. Your browser may warn that this type of file can harm your device; the file comes straight from this site.

2. Allow & install

Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap Install. You can turn that permission off again afterwards.

3. Sign in or register

Open CLARION. Patients sign in with their NRIC and password, or choose Register to create an account in a few minutes.
